Apollo 11 used the Saturn V SA-506 launch vehicle, consisting of the S-IC, S-II, and S-IVB stages. The S-IC first stage used five F-1 engines, while the S-II used five J-2 engines. The S-IVB provided the trans-lunar injection (TLI) burn. The Command Module Columbia used an Apollo Guidance Computer (AGC) for navigation and guidance, with the DSKY (Display and Keyboard) interface for crew input. The Lunar Module Eagle used the PGNCS (Primary Guidance, Navigation and Control System) and landing radar during descent. Communications with Mission Control used S-band telemetry, while the crew communicated through dedicated voice and biomedical telemetry channels.
