Astronauts experience an environment very different from life on Earth. In orbit, they experience microgravity, which makes ordinary activities such as eating, sleeping, and exercising more challenging. Astronauts living aboard space stations conduct scientific experiments, maintain equipment, and study how humans respond to long periods in space. Exercise is especially important because microgravity can cause muscles and bones to weaken. Astronauts must also carefully manage oxygen, water, food, and waste. Their experiences provide valuable information for future long-duration missions. Living in space requires extensive training, teamwork, discipline, and careful preparation for unexpected situations.
