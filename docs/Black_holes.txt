Black holes are among the most mysterious objects in the universe. They form when enormous amounts of matter become compressed into an extremely small region, creating gravity so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape after crossing the event horizon. Scientists cannot directly see black holes, but they can observe their effects on nearby stars, gas, and light. Some black holes are formed from massive stars, while supermassive black holes exist at the centers of many galaxies. Studying black holes helps scientists test theories about gravity, space, and time. They demonstrate how strange and extreme the universe can be.
