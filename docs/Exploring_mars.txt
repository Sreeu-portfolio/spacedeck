Mars is often called the Red Planet because iron minerals on its surface give it a reddish appearance. It has enormous volcanoes, deep valleys, dusty plains, and polar ice caps. Scientists have discovered evidence that liquid water existed on Mars in the distant past. Robotic spacecraft and rovers have been studying the planet for decades, collecting information about its rocks, atmosphere, and climate. One important goal is to determine whether Mars ever supported microbial life. Future missions may bring samples back to Earth and eventually send humans to Mars. Studying Mars can also teach us about planetary evolution.
