The possibility of life beyond Earth is one of the most exciting questions in space science. Scientists search for environments where liquid water, suitable temperatures, and important chemical ingredients might exist. Mars, Europa, and Enceladus are among the places studied because they may have conditions capable of supporting life. Astronomers also examine planets orbiting other stars, called exoplanets, looking for signs that their atmospheres could support living organisms. So far, there is no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial life. However, continued exploration and improved telescopes may eventually provide important clues about whether Earth is unique.
