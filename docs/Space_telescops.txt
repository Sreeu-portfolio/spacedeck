Space telescopes allow scientists to observe the universe without many of the limitations caused by Earth’s atmosphere. Telescopes such as Hubble and the James Webb Space Telescope have captured remarkable observations of galaxies, stars, nebulae, and distant planets. Because different telescopes detect different types of electromagnetic radiation, they reveal different aspects of cosmic objects. The James Webb Space Telescope can observe infrared light and study some of the earliest galaxies formed after the universe began. These observatories act like powerful windows into space, helping researchers investigate cosmic history and understand how galaxies, stars, and planetary systems develop.
