The future of space exploration may involve humans and robots traveling farther than ever before. Scientists and engineers are developing spacecraft, life-support systems, habitats, and other technologies for longer missions. The Moon could serve as an important location for scientific research and technology development. Mars is another major target for future exploration. Robotic missions may explore dangerous environments before humans arrive, while advanced telescopes will continue studying distant worlds. Space exploration could also improve our understanding of Earth and the universe. As technology advances, humanity may gradually expand its ability to explore and work beyond Earth.
