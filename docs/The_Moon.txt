The Moon is Earth’s closest natural neighbor in space and has fascinated humans for thousands of years. Its gravity influences ocean tides, while its phases have been used to measure time across many cultures. In 1969, Apollo 11 became the first mission to land humans on the Moon. Astronauts collected rocks and conducted scientific experiments there. Today, scientists continue studying the Moon because it preserves clues about the early history of the solar system. Lunar exploration may also help develop technologies needed for future missions to Mars and other destinations. The Moon remains an important stepping stone for exploration.
