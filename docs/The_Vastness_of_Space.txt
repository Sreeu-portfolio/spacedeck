Space is an enormous region beyond Earth’s atmosphere containing planets, stars, moons, asteroids, comets, and galaxies. Scientists believe the universe began billions of years ago and has continued expanding ever since. Our solar system is only a tiny part of the Milky Way galaxy, which itself contains billions of stars. Space is mostly empty, but it contains fascinating objects and powerful forces. Studying space helps scientists understand the origins of Earth, the formation of planets, and the evolution of stars. It also encourages technological development and inspires humans to explore places beyond our home planet.
