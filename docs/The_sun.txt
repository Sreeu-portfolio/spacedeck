The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system and provides almost all the energy needed for life on Earth. It is an enormous sphere of extremely hot plasma undergoing nuclear fusion in its core. The energy produced there travels outward and eventually reaches Earth as light and heat. Solar activity can produce powerful storms that affect satellites, radio communications, and electrical systems. Scientists continuously observe the Sun to understand its behavior and protect technology in space and on Earth. Although the Sun appears ordinary compared with some stars, its energy makes our planet habitable.
