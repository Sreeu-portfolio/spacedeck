Animals were sent into space before humans to study how living organisms respond to spaceflight. Dogs, monkeys, chimpanzees, mice, insects, turtles, and other animals helped scientists understand the effects of microgravity, acceleration, radiation, and confinement. Famous examples include Laika, the first animal to orbit Earth, and Ham, a chimpanzee whose flight helped demonstrate that humans could perform tasks in space. These missions played an important role in the development of human spaceflight.
